# Terk Energy: image brief

Nine image slots. Three concern people. Two of those have been removed from the
site pending your photography, and the third is retained with a caveat. Six are
**optional upgrades**, where the stock photograph is adequate but Terk's own work would be better.

## How to submit

Name each file **exactly** as the filename in the tables below and drop it into
`assets/img/`. The site picks it up with no code change, provided the shape is
close to the ratio given. If the ratio differs a lot, tell me and I will adjust
the crop in CSS.

Send me the real pixel dimensions of each file, or I will read them off the
files. The `width`/`height` attributes in the markup have to match the real file
or the page jumps as images load.

## Things that apply to every image

- **Everyone shown must be African.** This is the point of the exercise.
- **No other company's name, logo or livery may be legible** on hard hats,
  hi-vis, equipment, vessels or signage. This is a vendor's website; another
  operator's branding on it reads badly and can raise permission questions.
- **No watermarks**, and no images bought under a licence that forbids web use.
- **JPEG**, ideally under 600 KB each after export. The audience includes offices
  on poor connections.
- **Landscape unless the table says portrait.**
- **Every photograph is graded on the site**: desaturated about two thirds and
  pushed onto a steel-blue axis, so that ten unrelated photographs read as one
  set. Vivid colour is therefore wasted. What survives the grade is **contrast,
  clear silhouette and legible action**. A busy, evenly-lit frame goes muddy.
- **Real work beats posed work.** Hands doing something, in a real place, with
  real dirt on it. Avoid the white-background studio look and avoid anyone
  smiling at the camera holding a clipboard.
- If people are identifiable, make sure you have their permission to publish.

## Urgent: the three images with people

### 1. Safety briefing on site  *(supplied, closed)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **File** | `hsse.jpg` |
| **Used on** | Home, in the safety section. HSSE & Quality, as the page banner. |
| **Shape** | Portrait or square, at least 1400 x 1750. It is cropped wide for the banner and tall on the home page, so keep the subject central with room on all sides. |
| **Alt text in code** | "Two Terk Energy workers in personal protective equipment reviewing a checklist on site." |

**What to shoot or find:** two African workers in full PPE (hard hats, hi-vis,
gloves, safety glasses) in the middle of a genuine safety exchange. One holding a
permit, checklist or tablet; the other pointing at something out of frame or at
the document. A toolbox talk, a pre-job briefing, or a site inspection all work.

Outdoors on a real site is much better than an office. The point of the picture is
that safety is something people actually do here, not a policy statement, so
faces engaged with the task beat faces engaged with the camera.

### 4. Home page hero

| | |
|---|---|
| **File** | `hero-offshore.jpg` |
| **Used on** | Home, full screen behind the headline. The single most important image on the site. |
| **Shape** | Wide landscape, at least 2400 px wide. |
| **Alt text** | Decorative, no alt text. The headline carries the meaning. |

**What to shoot or find:** a Terk asset or operation with air around it. An
offshore installation, a vessel under way, a rig at dusk, a jetty at first light.

**Composition matters more here than anywhere else.** The headline and buttons sit
over the **left half**, on a heavy dark wash. So the subject must sit **right of
centre**, and the left third should be relatively empty: open sea, sky, haze. A
subject dead-centre will end up behind the type. Shoot wider than you think you
need; it is cropped hard on phones.

Dusk, dawn or overcast all work. Flat midday sun does not.
